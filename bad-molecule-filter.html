BAD Molecule Filter

A free machine learning tool by Abdallah Abou Hajal that predicts whether a small molecule will clump into a colloidal aggregate and show up as a false positive in a drug screen.

What it checks

  1. Colloidal aggregation

    Give it a molecule as a SMILES string. It predicts whether the molecule is likely to form colloidal aggregates, which inhibit proteins nonspecifically and produce false hits in high-throughput screening. It was Abdallah's MSc work at Al Ain University.

  2. What it does not check

    Aggregation only. It does not flag PAINS substructures or other kinds of assay interference, such as reactive or redox cycling compounds.
